A DA3 implementation of personal finance management using a property graph database.

Why a graph database?

A household's finances connect people, banks, employers and investments. Neo4j stores these connections directly. Cypher can match a person who works for a company and holds shares issued by it, or follow a household's members to their investments across companies.

Model and integrity

Household, User, Company, Bank, Account, Share, Subscription, Goal, Category and Transaction are node labels. AuditEvent stores permanent snapshots. Share ownership is a HOLDS relationship with a quantity property. Business IDs have unique keys; references are actual relationships.

Amounts are stored as integer paise. Each application write acquires a shared database lock before checking references and balances. Transaction changes, balance updates and audit events commit together or roll back together. This deliberately serializes writes for a small classroom application.

Neo4j Community supports uniqueness constraints. Required fields, allowed values, ownership cardinality, deletion rules and nonnegative balances are validated by the application. Direct edits through Neo4j Browser can bypass these application rules.
